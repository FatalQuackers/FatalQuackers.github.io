Do you also do scripting or UI design?
For commissions, no. I focus on what I'm best at: world building, character animation and QA playtesting. By not spreading myself thin over scripts and UI, you get the highest quality visual and testing work.
What payment methods do you accept?
Robux, via Roblox group payouts or a T-shirt with the 30% tax covered by the client. Direct cash equivalents are accepted depending on project scope. Open to rev-share only for established, highly promising studios.
